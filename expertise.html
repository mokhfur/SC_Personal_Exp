Professional background

Clinical Statistical Programming

Statistical programming turns collected clinical trial data into the datasets, tables, listings and figures that statisticians, clinicians and regulators review. Shafi's professional background lies in this work, and it remains the core discipline of the company he founded.

Hands-on and oversight

CDISC & Regulatory Standards

CDISC standards define how clinical data is structured for submission: SDTM organises collected data and ADaM supports analysis. Shafi has delivered CDISC and TLF programming for AstraZeneca and a full SDTM and ADaM submission package from legacy data, and oversees how standards-driven work is planned and delivered across his company.

Industry experience

Clinical Development

Across approximately 33 years in the pharmaceutical industry, Shafi has worked in an environment shaped by the requirements of clinical development: study design, data collection, analysis and reporting, and the expectations of the teams that depend on accurate outputs.

Hands-on and oversight

Programming Innovation

Shafi has a particular interest in how programming innovation can make clinical reporting more efficient without compromising quality. He has developed applications that auto-generate programs and cut programming time by 40%, a risk-based monitoring application used in mega trials, and endpoint and TLF macros reused across studies, and encourages the same approaches within the teams he leads.

Hands-on and oversight

PK/PD & Pharmacometric Data

Clinical pharmacology programming supports pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic analysis, including the preparation of datasets for population modelling in NONMEM. Shafi has generated NONMEM and CDISC datasets for multiple PK/PD projects for Bayer and Merck, and presented at the PAGE meeting on using large NONMEM datasets built from standard SDTM to accelerate model building.

Leadership

Business & Operations Leadership

As Founder and Managing Director since 2001, Shafi has been responsible for the direction of Shafi Consultancy Ltd.: building its programming operations, developing its people and maintaining working relationships with pharmaceutical companies and CROs internationally.
